# Highlights

> MDPI-style highlights: 3–5 concise bullet points (each ≤ 85 characters recommended).  
> Source: manuscript Sections 1–4, verified results from `docs/FINAL_RESULTS.md`.

1. Dual-expert on-device AI fuses arrhythmia beat classification and myocardial infarction detection via OR rule on an ESP32-S3 microcontroller — previously unreported at MCU scale.

2. Deployment-chain-matched training quantifies and corrects a causal-versus-zero-phase filter mismatch costing up to ΔAUC −0.105 on the PTB domain.

3. Patient-level multi-dataset evaluation across MIT-BIH, INCART, and PTB with an explicit leakage audit and per-class AAMI EC57 breakdown.

4. Complete, low-cost, reproducible stack: PEDOT:PSS flexible dry electrode → AD8232 AFE → dual-core ESP32-S3 inference → BLE → Flutter smartphone application.

5. Per-class AAMI reporting exposes fusion-beat recall ceiling (0.442) that aggregate AUC hides — in the spirit of limitation-aware computer-interpreted ECG evaluation.
